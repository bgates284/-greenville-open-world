# Vehicle models

The detailed cars in the game. Each was converted for the game (turned to face forward, wheels split
into separately turning parts, paint made recolourable, compressed with meshopt and WebP textures;
`*_lod.glb` are simplified copies used for traffic). The original authors keep their copyright; the
files here are shared under the same licences.
